# Game artwork prompts

The ten wallpapers in `wallpapers/` were generated and revised with the built-in image generation tool. The ten square icons in `icons/` are hand-authored SVGs. Artwork is not wired into the app. The prompts below the revision section created the initial versions; the revision prompts describe the current wallpapers.

## Natura scientific revision

The current Natura wallpaper uses the preceding Natura PNG as its edit target with this final prompt:

> Use case: style-transfer. Edit the supplied Natura game-card wallpaper only. Turn the current fox-and-small-bird meadow scene into a scientific natural-history field-guide illustration. Keep the fox and bird anatomically believable with species-appropriate proportions and plumage/fur markings, rendered as precise clean shapes rather than cute cartoon faces. Make the composition feel like an observational biology plate: restrained botanical specimens, a few faint habitat contour lines and subtle measuring ticks around the animals, balanced specimen layout with the fox and bird clearly visible at small card size. Use the Natura SVG icon color language: dark forest green #1d4139, leaf green #82bd82, muted cream, warm ochre and restrained fox orange. Flat editorial illustration with a little layered shading, more detailed than the SVG icon but much simpler than a realistic painting. Preserve the wide 16:9 full-bleed format and center-crop safety. No text, labels, numbers, logos, UI, frame or watermark.

## Current wallpaper revision

Each current wallpaper used its initial PNG as an edit target and this shared prompt, followed by the game-specific sentence below:

> Use case: style-transfer. The supplied image is the existing wallpaper and edit target. Redraw its central game concept as a polished minimal vector-inspired 16:9 game card illustration matching the simple square SVG icon aesthetic: smooth geometric silhouettes, crisp edges, solid fills, 3 clear depth layers, a restrained 4-5-color palette, and subtle two-tone shadows. Retain more scene detail than a single app icon, but remove photographic or 3D realism, fine textures, tiny objects, dramatic lighting and clutter. Keep the main subject easy to recognize at small card size and safe for a short central crop. Full bleed landscape; no text, logos, UI, frame or watermark.
