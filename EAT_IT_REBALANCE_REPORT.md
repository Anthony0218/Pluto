# Eat It rebalance

This extends the existing shared simulation, procedural Three.js canvas renderer, menus, settings store, multiplayer authority and JSON result ledger. Existing unrelated working-tree edits were preserved.

1. **Hell geometry:** 1,600 × 1,120 becomes **2,880 × 1,920**, a **3.086× area** increase (208.6% more area). The platform has 864 actual destructible cells instead of 280. Spawns, navigation, helpers, lava, camera bounds and minimap use this geometry. The camera follows the participant so enlarged characters remain readable.
2. **Eruptions:** one or two safe patches warn at 30 seconds, then every seeded 27–33 seconds. Two seconds of pulsing cracked floor, pressure and sparks precede a 1.2-second eruption. A character is hit at most once per eruption, receiving 150 units of knockback over 0.4 seconds. Eruptions never directly kill or remove growth; displaced characters can fall through the existing lava system.
3. **Match clock:** persisted **2 / 10 / 20 minutes**, default **10**. The authority chooses Hell transition or the existing normal size/score result rules at timeout. Old closing-ring behavior does not cut configured longer matches short.
4. **Durations:** Shield **30 seconds**, Magnet **15**, Speed **15**, activated 2x **20**. Existing effect deadlines remain authoritative and appear in the HUD. Magnet still respects eligibility and excludes buildings.
5. **Removed items:** Temporary Size and generic Growth Boost no longer spawn, render, collect, affect geometry or appear in the HUD/localization. Old snapshot item/timer keys are discarded safely.
6. **Stored 2x:** pickup stores one charge. **USE 2x NOW** activates it manually. A duplicate remains in the world when the player already has a stored charge. One replacement charge can be stored during an active window, but cannot extend that window.
7. **Earned growth:** activation never doubles existing mass. Every eligible reward uses the shared growth helper; expiration removes only the future multiplier. All earned mass remains, including player, building, Pluto and friendly-food rewards.
8. **Divider:** `/2` immediately sets future reward efficiency to 0.5 without shrinking current mass. Repeated dividers do not stack. It persists for the current life and clears on death/standard-size respawn, and on the existing Hell survival reset. With active 2x, the combined modifier is exactly 1×.
9. **Rarity:** 2x has a 2% weight and divider 1% per five-second spawn attempt; neither is present initially. Each has a one-world-item cap and a respective 60/90-second minimum spawn cooldown. Invalid spawn positions can reduce realized frequency.
10. **Hell Speed:** attempts every 12 seconds, with at most three ground items. Reachability and safe-ground checks reject disconnected, destroyed or warning ground, eruption areas and black-hole proximity. Destroyed-ground pickups retire. Duration remains 15 seconds.
11. **Black hole:** 16% slow, 60% normal, 20% fast, 4% extreme, with 0.7/1/1.55/2.7 speed factors. Twelve percent of attacks are smooth quadratic curves. Extreme attacks warn for two seconds; others 1.1. Paths and speed categories lock before warning. One optional 0.5–1.5-second mid-sweep pause is sampled during travel. Floor destruction and swirling visuals continue while paused. Renderer, bots and simulation share the path function.
12. **Leaderboard:** current displayed Growth descending, then lives descending, then stable seat order for presentation only. It updates at the existing 10 Hz HUD rate. Actual normal-match victory rules retain their existing score tiebreaker.
13. **Review:** aggregated object-kind counts, reused world-model thumbnails, power icons and localized names. Includes total earned growth, 2x pickups/activations, dividers, hostile hits, and existing placement, max size, lives, Pluto, animal and Hell statistics. Historical rows remain unchanged; missing new fields default safely.
14. **Friendly feeding:** the animal circles the recipient and sends an actual apple from its current position toward the mouth over 0.65 seconds. The normal fall/consumption event grants growth afterward. Pigeons drop from above; cats stay on the ground and toss food.
15. **Hostile pigeon:** larger red-eyed form circles, dives toward the face, hits, then retreats. No direct growth loss.
16. **Hostile cat:** approaches on the ground, briefly jumps toward the face, scratches, then returns to circling. No flying follow state and no growth loss.
17. **Stun:** one second per successful hit, with attacks at 5, 10, 15 and 20 seconds of the hostile interval. Hostility ends after 20 seconds. Existing longer stuns are combined with `max`, not accumulated; controls recover automatically. Eyes, rim shaking, stars and scratch marks communicate injury.
18. **Growth UI:** persistent Growth value uses the same mass-derived display as rankings. Comic popups carry the actual shared reward delta, including 2x and `/2`; modifier/activation notices are distinct. At most six popups render, each for 1.4 seconds, from the bounded event buffer without per-frame React state.
19. **Bots and authority:** Easy uses stored growth early; Medium waits for nearby meals; Hard waits for clusters, valuable food or friendly feeding. Hard/Medium steer away from dividers. Hell decisions consider public path segments, floor, revealed eruption warnings and Speed items. Future RNG/pauses/eruption locations are not consulted. Clients send activation intent; server membership, charge, phase and replay checks determine the result.
20. **Verification:** final command results are recorded below. Browser inspection covered manual activation/countdown, icon-only items, Growth/leaderboard/effect HUD, actual-model review thumbnails, Hell geometry/camera, curved warning, eruption warning/jet, pigeon feeding and its +15 reward, grounded cat feeding, pigeon/cat face attacks and stun feedback. Deterministic tests also cover extreme speeds, pauses, authority, rarity, expiry, legacy item cleanup, ledger compatibility and staggered final-fall ties.
21. **Limits:** remote deployment and live authenticated cross-device testing were not performed. Deploy the frontend and shared-import `eat-it-match` Edge Function together. The existing result/settings migration in this working tree persists all new JSON fields; no additional migration is needed. The existing Hell reset to starting mass is preserved; Hell bodies use twice the normal starting radius. Existing high-mass visual caps remain. Non-English/German/Spanish/Portuguese languages use the established fallback for new labels. Repository-wide Vite chunk-size warnings remain unrelated to this change.
