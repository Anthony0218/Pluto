# Copyright and licensing release review

Reviewed 6–7 October 2026. Scope: installed dependency metadata and shipped
notices, the files under `public`, vendored Go code, asset credits and data
import scripts, football/music provenance notes, and representative card/scene
images. `MEDIA_INVENTORY.csv` lists media paths, SHA-256 hashes and recorded
provenance status. This is a repository audit with primary-source terms checks,
not a guarantee that every asset is cleared or a jurisdiction-specific opinion.

## Image provenance supplied by the owner

On 7 October 2026 the owner identified `public/images` and confirmed: **AI
generated by you** (Codex). This covers that directory and its subdirectories,
including card illustrations and game/scene images. The media inventory records
this owner confirmation against the exact hashes in `AI_IMAGE_PROVENANCE.json`;
new or changed images revert to unrecorded status until separately confirmed.
Original prompts, generation history and any uploaded
reference permissions should be retained where available. The confirmation
does not extend to `public/sounds`, `public/models`, `public/MedievalKingdoms`,
`public/swag` or icons at the root of `public`.

Generated origin resolves the earlier missing-provenance finding for that
folder, but is not blanket rights clearance: recognizable third-party marks,
characters, copyrighted reference material and substantially similar output
can still matter. Sampled card/scene images do not establish an exhaustive
visual-similarity review of every generated asset. OpenAI's output ownership
terms are subject to applicable law and do not guarantee uniqueness or clear
someone else's rights. See [Europe terms](https://openai.com/policies/eu-terms-of-use/).

Stockfish has GPL obligations when distributed to browsers. Do not assume
that adding a credit licenses an integrated combined work: whether the app is
a separate aggregate or a combined program, and the scope of required source,
need review against the actual distribution. No app-wide GPL clearance is
claimed here. Preserve source access, license notices and modification records.
Sources: [Stockfish distribution guidance](https://stockfishchess.org/about/),
[release source](https://github.com/nmrugg/stockfish.js/tree/93c994592dcf3b4b21052ab925e9b534df9c0918),
[GNU aggregation guidance](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-faq.en.html#MereAggregation).

## Other reviewed sources

- The World Factbook text and facts bundled for Atlas History Battle
  (`data/geography/history.json`) are a US Government work in the public domain.
  The credits name the source as a courtesy and state that the CIA does not
  endorse the game; the CIA seal and name must not be used to suggest otherwise.
- GeoNames and World Bank data require attribution, license links and change
  indications. The credits identify selection/normalization and observation
  years. Preserve these and the source metadata on new exports; individual
  datasets can carry additional terms.
- Natural Earth and structured Wikidata are credited as public domain/CC0.
  Lichess puzzle/opening data, KataGo weights, MIT Web KaTrain adaptations,
  TensorFlow, fonts and flags retain the license distinctions in the credits.
- Football names and a small set of manually curated historical facts use
  provenance links; official emblems, photos, footage and trophy imagery are
  excluded. Original paraphrases, diagrams and synthesized teaching audio are
  documented in `FOOTBALL_DATA_AND_MEDIA.md`. Names can still raise trademark
  questions if presentation implies endorsement; no blanket trademark
  clearance is claimed. Don't add copied media or bulk provider datasets
  based solely on attribution or educational purpose.
- No sample recordings or copied scores were found in the music studio.
- The group-avatar README records image generation and generic subject
  prompts. That record is not clearance for independently protected marks or
  recognizable third-party characters in any future output.

Keep asset-specific permissions, download records, license versions and
authorship/generation records outside this audit too. A missing record can be
resolved by evidence of rights; this review does not presume infringement.
The current checkout is **not cleared for production on the strength of credits
alone** while the findings above remain unresolved.
