# Eat It: Jump, Strike, swallowing and companions

Implemented by extending the existing shared simulation, Three.js renderer/Canvas overlay, power-up array, stored-growth/ability controls, authoritative room commands and review. No replacement game or parallel physics/inventory system was added.

1. **Jump behavior.** Hell-only pickup stores one charge. Activation travels forward 320 world units over 0.95 seconds and rises up to 125 units. Direction comes from current movement, otherwise facing. Ground support is suspended only while airborne; the exact endpoint is checked on landing. Missing ground causes a fall without teleporting to safety. Airborne actors cannot collect, eat or be eaten.
2. **Jump controls and rarity.** Press **J** or its HUD button. Hell tries spawning every 12 seconds: 20% Jump, 10% Strike and otherwise Speed. Jump has a 24-second spawn cooldown and one active world pickup; Hell has a three-item total cap. Candidates must be reachable, intact floor away from players, black holes and eruption areas. Jump never appears in normal-map spawn tables.
3. **Car/house underpasses.** Supported vehicles and raised buildings remain passable until their actual mouth-fit threshold. Removed the height cutoff and wheel/stilt collision path that created the blocking gap. Bots use the same rule; genuinely solid props retain collisions.
4. **Devour transition.** Fitting cars/houses retain the existing approximately 25% entry threshold, supported-edge lean, increasing rotation, depth occlusion and 1.15-second full-size fall. They do not push the player away at the threshold or gain ordinary pre-entry suction.
5. **Tree choking.** Trunk fit and contact begin an upright downward intake. The canopy reaches the lip after 0.65 seconds. An oversized canopy then sticks visibly and locks voluntary movement for three seconds. An oversized trunk never starts entry. A fully fitting tree finishes swallowing in 1.5 seconds without choking. The shared opening radius matches the rendered mouth.
6. **Tree spit-out.** The 0.85-second sequence first raises the tree out of the mouth, then sends it through a short local tumbling arc. Mouth compression accompanies release. It settles back into one ordinary world object with a brief reacquisition cooldown and no growth reward.
7. **Burps.** Removed the visible word and its obsolete localization entries. Periodic puffs, eye response and rim motion remain cosmetic.
8. **Strike behavior.** Pickup stores one use separately from Jump and 2x Growth. Activation has a 0.06-second windup and a decelerating forward burst, nominally 180 units over 0.45 seconds. It uses existing ground movement/collisions, with rim motion and orange trails. Normal-map spawn weight is 4% per five-second attempt, with a 35-second cooldown and one active world pickup; no initial Strike pickups. Hell may also spawn it rarely.
9. **Strike control.** Press **Space** or its HUD button. Key repeats cannot reactivate a stored charge. Ready controls disappear when consumed; conflicting states disable activation. The item legend shows both new icons and localized tooltips.
10. **Strike devours.** The existing 130% radius rule, mouth fit, front contact, inward motion and shield protection remain authoritative. Strike adds positioning, never size or eating permission. It has no airborne lava protection. Dead, respawning, choking, falling, stunned, escaping, transitioning and already-active actors cannot start either ability; Jump is additionally restricted to Hell. Animal Escape and the new motions cannot overlap.
11. **Bots.** Normal bots use Strike on valid prey lines; Hard bots also use it for retreat. Difficulty changes timing, targeting tolerance and decision frequency. Hell bots assess public floor/warning geometry and sampled landing endpoints for Jump; Easy bots may spend it earlier. Grounded Hell escape Strikes require a sampled safe path. Bots consume the same charges and use the same activation checks as players.
12. **Friendship.** Both completed quests now grant **60 seconds**. Expiry stops new feeding, clears helper entitlement/target/feed timers and triggers the normal leaving animation. Existing valid friendship carries into Hell under the established rules: pigeon assist five seconds, cat assist two seconds. Normal Escape timing and single-use behavior remain.
13. **Following and feeding.** Companions circle their owner and catch up according to player speed and distance. Friendly cats can traverse supported underpasses. Player-relative scaling remains. Every four seconds before expiry, an apple visibly travels from the companion into the existing mouth/reward flow; there are fourteen possible feeds, subject to the existing object cap. The HUD shows one compact heart/countdown label. Completion text is brief, and the Escape button shows readiness instead of duplicating the countdown.
14. **Authority, review and localization.** Authenticated room operations accept only activation intent for the requesting member. Pickup, ownership, motion, contact, timer and rewards stay in shared simulation; clients interpolate ability transforms only. Review adds paired collected/used counts, successful gap jumps, Strike-assisted devours, companion seconds/feeds and vehicles eaten alongside existing tree/choke/building stats. All new labels are present in English, German, Bavarian, Korean, Russian, Spanish and Portuguese. World motion remains outside React; HUD refresh stays at 10 Hz.
15. **Verification.** The focused new file passes 25 behavior tests. The complete Eat It suite passes **224/224**, and the full project suite passes **401/401**, without skips. TypeScript, production build, ESLint on Eat It source/UI and the modified TSX fixture, test-file lint and `git diff --check` pass. The build retains the existing >500 kB chunk warning. Browser checks covered trunk entry, canopy sticking, reverse-out, near-threshold car/house underpasses, house leaning, Jump across a visible lava gap and landing, actual J/Space controls and consumed HUD charges, grounded Strike trails, both item icons, particle-only burps, companions still present at 59 simulated seconds with fourteen feeds, compact companion UI and a 390×844 mobile layout. Browser error logs were empty. Additional full-world companion runs stayed close to moving players on both maps; capped worlds skipped some feeding attempts as designed.
16. **Remaining limits and deployment.** Motion and prop collisions remain deterministic approximations rather than a general rigid-body engine. Jump ends where aimed and can fail; companions respect existing world bounds and feeding caps. Hosted multi-human latency/load testing was not performed. **This update needs no new SQL migration** because the existing room snapshots and results retain state/stats JSON. **Redeploy `eat-it-match` and the frontend together** to use the new shared imports and `jump`/`strike` operations online. The earlier Eat It result/settings migrations still need applying if they are not already deployed. No remote schema check or deployment was performed. Existing unrelated working-tree changes were preserved.

Reproducible development fixtures: `tests/eat-it-abilities-preview.html` and the `abilities`, `feeding` and `cat feeding` scenarios in `tests/eat-it-ui-preview.html`. These are not production routes.
